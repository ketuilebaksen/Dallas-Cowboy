And let me explain the specific problem he's fixing, because it's one of the most important concepts in modern defense and almost nobody explains it.

In a lot of coverages, responsibilities flip based on the offensive formation. If the tight end lines up on the left, one linebacker has him. If he motions to the right, a different linebacker has him, and the first one now has the running back instead.

That flip has to be recognized by both players, simultaneously, in the two or three seconds before the snap.

When one of them sees it and the other doesn't, you get exactly what we watched on Sunday. Either two defenders covering the same man, or nobody covering the man who's about to catch a touchdown.
