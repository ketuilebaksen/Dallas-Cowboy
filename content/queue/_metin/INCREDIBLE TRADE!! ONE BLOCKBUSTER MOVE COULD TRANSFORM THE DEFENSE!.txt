Let me explain why those are genuinely different jobs, because fans lump them together and they shouldn't.

A downhill thumper reads the run, fills the gap, and delivers a blow. That's about instincts, physicality, and taking on blockers. Those skills age reasonably well, because recognition improves as speed declines and the two partially cancel out.

Coverage linebacker is a different sport. That's turning and running with a tight end, carrying a back up the seam, changing direction in space against a receiver who knows where he's going and you don't. That skill is almost purely athletic, and it's the first one to go.

Bobby Wagner is still an excellent run defender. Asking him to be a three-down coverage answer at this stage might be asking for something nobody could deliver.
