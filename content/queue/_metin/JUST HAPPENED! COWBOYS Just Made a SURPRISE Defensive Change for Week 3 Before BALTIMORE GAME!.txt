Let me explain what a stinger is, because the name makes it sound minor and it isn't.

A stinger, sometimes called a burner, happens when the nerves running from the neck through the shoulder get compressed or stretched, usually on a hard collision. The result is a sudden shock of pain down the arm, often with numbness or weakness in the hand.

It's called a stinger because it feels like an electric shock. Players describe losing feeling in their arm for a stretch of time.

Now picture a rookie linebacker with a dead arm, in his first game wearing the radio, going back onto the field to run a defense.
