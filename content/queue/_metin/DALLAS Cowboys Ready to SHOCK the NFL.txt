Now the blitz, because this is the thing I’d be most worried about and the video names the cause.

I did the geometry of that nine days ago and it hasn’t changed.

Pressure takes defenders out of the middle of the field, because the middle is where linebackers live and linebackers are the cheapest blitzers. So a blitz creates space behind it, not beside it.
