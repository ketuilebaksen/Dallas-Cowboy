Game-level balance does not resolve a personnel-level tell.

Dallas could have run the football on one hundred percent of its snaps from heavy personnel, and thrown the football on seventy percent of its snaps from normal personnel, and the box score would look exactly like a balanced offense while the tell remained perfect.

Because the tell isn’t about whether a team runs and passes. Every team runs and passes. The tell is about whether a specific grouping of eleven men predicts what’s coming.

A defensive coordinator doesn’t game-plan against a season total. He game-plans against a tendency chart, and a tendency chart is organised by personnel grouping and formation, not by game. If that chart says six-lineman personnel equals run on forty out of forty snaps, then Arizona will load the box every time it sees that grouping, no matter how balanced Dallas looked on Monday.
