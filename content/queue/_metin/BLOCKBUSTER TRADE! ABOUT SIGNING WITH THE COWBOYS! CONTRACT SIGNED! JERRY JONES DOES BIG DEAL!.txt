Here's why an interior player like that matters so much, and this is the mechanism most fans never get told.

Edge pressure comes from outside. A quarterback feels it, steps up into the pocket, and buys himself another second. Every quarterback in this league is coached to do exactly that.

Interior pressure comes from directly in front of his face. There's nowhere to step up to. The escape hatch is gone. He either throws it before he's ready or he starts moving sideways, and moving sideways is how a quarterback eats a sack from the edge rusher who was already beaten.
