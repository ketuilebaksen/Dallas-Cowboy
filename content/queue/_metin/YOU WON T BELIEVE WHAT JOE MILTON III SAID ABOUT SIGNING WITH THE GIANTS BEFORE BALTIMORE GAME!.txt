Now, the rule. Because this is the part almost nobody explains, and it's the reason a story like this isn't automatically nonsense.

Practice squad players are not protected property.

Let me say that again, because it surprises people. If a player is on your practice squad, any other team in the league can sign him. They don't need your permission. They don't have to trade for him. They don't owe you anything.

There's one condition. The team signing him has to put him on their active fifty-three man roster, not on their own practice squad. That's the whole guardrail. You can't poach somebody just to stash him. You have to actually promote him.

And typically that comes with a guaranteed salary commitment for a stretch of weeks, which is the financial disincentive that keeps teams from doing it casually.

But here's the part that turns this into an actual weekly decision for Dallas.

Teams are allowed to protect a small number of practice squad players each week from being signed away. Four of them. Every week, the front office submits a list, and the players on that list cannot be poached for that week.

Four. That's it.

So if Dallas has, say, sixteen players on its practice squad, twelve of them are available to anybody in the league at any moment during that week.

Which means the real question in this rumor isn't whether the Giants are allowed to sign Joe Milton. They are. The question is whether Dallas is putting him on the protected list.
