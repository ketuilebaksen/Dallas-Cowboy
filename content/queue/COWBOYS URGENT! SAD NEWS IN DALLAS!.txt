Now here’s why that sentence is the most informative one in nine minutes.

When a player goes on injured reserve during the season, he cannot simply walk back onto the field. The team has to designate him for return, which starts a twenty-one day clock during which he’s allowed to practise. Within those twenty-one days they either activate him to the roster or he’s done for the year.
