When a team signs a player to a big extension, most of the guaranteed money comes as a signing bonus. That bonus is paid up front, but for salary cap purposes it gets spread out across the years of the deal. That's called proration.

Now here's the trap. If you trade or release that player before the deal ends, all of the remaining prorated bonus money accelerates. It all slams onto your cap at once.
